The Sun

The main part of our solar system. It is a yellow small star that accounts for 98% of the mass in the entire solar system. Its gravity holds everything together.

Earth & Moon:

The third planet from the sun and the only planet known to support life. It is orbited by a satellite, the moon, and various artificial space probes

Saturn:

The sixth planet from the sun and the second-largest planet in the solar system. It is famous for its stunning and complex ring system, which is made mostly of ice particles and rocks.
