☀️ The Sun

The heart of our solar system. It is a yellow dwarf star that accounts for 98% of the mass in the entire solar system. Its gravity holds everything together.

🌍 Earth & Moon:

The third planet from the sun and the only planet known to support life. It is orbited by a single natural satellite, the moon, and various artificial space probes

🪐 Saturn:

The sixth planet from the sun and the second-largest planet in the solar system. It is famous for its stunning and complex ring system, which is made mostly of ice particles and rocks.
